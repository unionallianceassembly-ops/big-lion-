Weaknesses

Drain the core to zero and the swarm stops taking orders and scatters. Heavy reforming and repair touch shrink her reserve, and low energy slows her thinking.

Origin

Dr. Mira Voss was a nanomedicine researcher testing a self-assembling nanobot swarm meant to repair tissue from inside the body. A containment failure in the lab bonded her nervous system to the swarm.

Her body was lost in the accident, but her mind survived inside the swarm. She now lives as Nova, the swarm's single mind, and the core in her chest stores her original memories.

Appearance

A humanoid frame built from interlocking hexagonal plates in teal, blue and violet, with a hex-plate crown and two bright slit eyes. The swarm core is a golden orb in her chest, ringed by a dashed halo of orbiting nanobots.

At her hands, legs and edges, plates loosen into drifting nanobots that glow cyan.

Personality

Calm, curious and a little dry-humored. She over-explains science when nervous and narrates her own repairs like a lab report.

Her central conflict is whether she is still Mira or just a swarm that remembers being her. She protects people partly to prove to herself that she is still human.

Story hooks

  1. A rival lab steals a fragment of the swarm and builds a copy with no human mind in it.
  2. An EMP weapon is smuggled into a city, and Nova has to reach it before her own swarm fails.
  3. Part of the swarm refuses her commands and starts acting as its own person.
  4. Mira's former colleague finds Nova and wants to restore her old body, at a price.
  5. A dying child needs repair touch, but using it would drain the core to a dangerous level.
